# Retail Business Intelligence DataMart

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the design and implementation of a Retail Sales DataMart for business intelligence, analytics, and sales forecasting.

The solution integrates MySQL, SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI concepts to support decision-making through KPI analysis and predictive analytics.

## Business Objective

Transform raw sales transactions into actionable business insights.

Key business questions:

* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which customer segments are most profitable?
* Which cities and stores perform best?
* What are the monthly sales trends?
* What are the expected future sales?
